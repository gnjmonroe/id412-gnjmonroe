 Summary of Lecture 
 Over  the  past  50  years,  interactions  between  humans,  software,  machines,  and  environments  have 
 transformed  how  we  create,  experience,  and  understand  space.  Digital  technologies  now  allow  designers  and 
 artists  to  build  expressive,  participatory,  and  real-time  media  environments  in  which  space  responds  to  human 
 action. 
 This  course  explores  interactive  space  as  the  design  of  relationships  between  user  behaviour,  real-time 
 systems,  and  physical  or  digital  environments.  Students  will  study  key  critical  concepts  in  interactive  media, 
 ranging  from  embodied  interaction  and  spatial  interfaces  to  camera-based  input,  data  visualisation,  generative 
 content, and interactive narrative. 
 As  a  final  outcome,  students  will  produce  a  creative  project  that  demonstrates  the  technical,  aesthetic,  and 
 conceptual  dimensions  of  interactive  space.  The  goal  is  to  build  a  meaningful  dialogue  between  user  and  space 
 through interaction. Students will work both individually and collaboratively throughout the term. 
 NOTE* Course Prerequisite 
 1.  Taking the 'Immersive Space' course in the spring semester is recommended before taking this class. 
 2.  Priority enrollment is given to undergraduate students majoring in Industrial Design. 
 3.  Graduate students in Industrial Design are also given priority. 
 Prerequisites  :  Participants  should  understand  the  basics  of  HTML,  CSS,  JavaScript,  and  React.  If  you  are 
 not  familiar  with  these  frontend  coding  frameworks,  it  is  highly  recommended  to  complete  some  basic 
 JavaScript tutorials online beforehand. 
 During this course, you will learn: 
 1.  Applying methods and technologies for creating audio-visual, interaction-based media environments. 
 2.  Conducting  critical  and  contextual  research  on  interactive  projects,  with  attention  to  how  interactivity 
 connects content, user behaviour, real-time systems, social critique, and physical or digital space. 
 3.  Designing  and  prototyping  creative  and  speculative  real-time  interactions  that  connect  user  behaviour, 
 computational systems, and physical or digital space. 
 4.  Developing  experimental  game-engine-based,  web-based,  and  vision-based  interactive  content  for 
 monitor, projection, and spatial installation settings.
