​Summary of Lecture​

​ ver​ ​the​ ​past​ ​50​ ​years,​ ​interactions​ ​between​ ​humans,​ ​software,​ ​machines,​ ​and​ ​environments​ ​have​
O
​transformed​​how​​we​​create,​​experience,​​and​​understand​​space.​​Digital​​technologies​​now​​allow​​designers​
 ​and​​artists​​to​​build​​expressive,​​participatory,​​and​​real-time​​media​​environments​​in​​which​​space​​responds​
  ​to human action.​
   ​This​ ​course​ ​explores​ ​interactive​ ​space​ ​as​ ​the​​design​​of​​relationships​​between​​user​​behaviour,​​real-time​
    ​systems,​ ​and​ ​physical​ ​or​ ​digital​ ​environments.​ ​Students​ ​will​ ​study​ ​key​ ​critical​ ​concepts​ ​in​ ​interactive​
     ​media,​ ​ranging​ ​from​ ​embodied​ ​interaction​ ​and​ ​spatial​ ​interfaces​ ​to​ ​camera-based​ ​input,​ ​data​
      ​visualisation, generative content, and interactive narrative.​
       ​As​ ​a​ ​final​ ​outcome,​ ​students​ ​will​​produce​​a​​creative​​project​​that​​demonstrates​​the​​technical,​​aesthetic,​
        ​and​​conceptual​​dimensions​​of​​interactive​​space.​​The​​goal​​is​​to​​build​​a​​meaningful​​dialogue​​between​​user​
         ​and​ ​space​ ​through​ ​interaction.​ ​Students​ ​will​ ​work​ ​both​ ​individually​ ​and​ ​collaboratively​ ​throughout​ ​the​
          ​term.​

​During this course, you will learn:​
    ​1.​ ​Applying​ ​methods​ ​and​ ​technologies​ ​for​ ​creating​ ​audio-visual,​ ​interaction-based​ ​media​
          ​environments.​
    ​2.​ ​Conducting​ ​critical​ ​and​ ​contextual​ ​research​ ​on​ ​interactive​ ​projects,​ ​with​ ​attention​ ​to​ ​how​
          ​interactivity​ ​connects​ ​content,​​user​​behaviour,​​real-time​​systems,​​social​​critique,​​and​​physical​​or​
          ​digital space.​
    ​3.​ ​Designing​ ​and​ ​prototyping​ ​creative​ ​and​ ​speculative​ ​real-time​ ​interactions​ ​that​ ​connect​ ​user​
          ​behaviour, computational systems, and physical or digital space.​
    ​4.​ ​Developing​ ​experimental​ ​game-engine-based,​​web-based,​​and​​vision-based​​interactive​​content​
          ​for monitor, projection, and spatial installation settings.​
